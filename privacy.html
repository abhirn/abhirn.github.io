Privacy policy

Stone Age Self Publisher · last updated 16 November 2026

Stone Age Self Publisher ("the app") is a private command-line tool. It is used only by the owner of the Stone Age Self YouTube channel, signed in with their own Google account. It is not offered to the public.

What data the app accesses

Through the YouTube API Services, and only for the owner's own channel, the app:

  • uploads videos and sets their title, description, tags, thumbnail and captions (YouTube Data API);
  • posts one comment on the owner's own videos (YouTube Data API);
  • reads the channel's list of uploads and their public statistics (YouTube Data API);
  • reads the channel's own analytics: views, watch time, audience retention and traffic sources (YouTube Analytics API).

The app does not access any other user's data, and it does not request personal information about viewers. YouTube Analytics data is aggregated and contains no individual viewer identities.

How the data is used and stored

Data is used only to publish the owner's videos and to write private performance reports that help improve future videos. Reports and the Google sign-in token are stored only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Retention and deletion

Local reports are kept until the owner deletes them. Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account security page at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which also stops the app from accessing any data.

Google and YouTube

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service; see also the Google Privacy Policy. The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Questions about this app or this policy: contact the channel owner through the About page of youtube.com/@StoneAgeSelf.
