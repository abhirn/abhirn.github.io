Stone Age Self

Your body lives in 2026. Your brain still lives 200,000 years ago.

Stone Age Self is a YouTube channel of animated stick-figure explainers about why modern humans do what they do, built on named, published studies. Watch it at youtube.com/@StoneAgeSelf.

Stone Age Self Publisher

Stone Age Self Publisher is a private tool used only by the channel's owner. It uploads the channel's own finished videos to YouTube after the owner reviews and approves each one, and reads the channel's own YouTube Analytics so future videos can improve. It has no other users.
